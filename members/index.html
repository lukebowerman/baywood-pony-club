Staying in the loop

TeamSnap

Baywood uses TeamSnap to keep everyone organized. It's a free app where you'll find the club calendar, meeting details and messages from club leaders, all in one place.

  • Schedule: meetings, lessons, rallies and camp, with times and locations. You can sync it to your phone's calendar.
  • Availability: let us know whether your rider can make each event.
  • Messages: club announcements and reminders arrive as notifications.
  • Roster: contact info for other club families.

Getting started

  1. Watch for an invitation email from TeamSnap and accept it.
  2. Download the TeamSnap app below.
  3. Sign in with the same email address the invitation went to.

Help us grow

Follow and tag Baywood

Every follow, like, share and tag helps more local families find us. It only takes a minute.

Facebook

  1. Open our page, facebook.com/BaywoodPC, and tap Follow (and Like if you see it).
  2. When you post about Pony Club, type @Baywood Pony Club and pick our page from the list so it links to us.
  3. Tap Share on our posts, especially event and recruiting posts, so your friends see them.
Open Facebook

Instagram

  1. Open our profile, @baywood_ponyclub, and tap Follow.
  2. On your posts, tap Tag people and add @baywood_ponyclub, or mention it in your caption or Story.
  3. Add #BaywoodPonyClub and #PonyClub, and reshare our posts to your Story.
