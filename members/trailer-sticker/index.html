Trailer stickers

Show your Baywood colors on the road. Every trailer that carries one helps local families find the club.

Main decal

Two designs to choose from

Both are 18 × 18 inches with rounded corners, sized to fit on one rear door of a two-horse trailer.

To scale

How it looks on a trailer

Here's the 18-inch decal on the rear door of a white two-horse bumper pull. It fits between the window and the bottom of the door with room to spare.

Measure your door first. On many trailers the latch handle and hinges leave about 18 to 20 inches of flat space below the window.

Smaller spaces

Small stickers

The name, city and web address from the main decal, for fenders, tack room doors, truck windows or anywhere a big square won't fit.

Ordering your own

  1. Download the print file for the design and size you want.
  2. Order a car door decal from Vistaprint (or any sign shop) and upload the file.
  3. Choose white vinyl, set the size to match the file, and pick a custom shape for the rounded corners. Check that the cut line in the preview follows the edge of the design.
  4. Clean the door with rubbing alcohol before you apply it, and smooth it on from the center out.
